# Chapter 2.2

Myra didn't return until later that evening. This time, she had a couple of others accompanying her.

One was an older lady, presumably Myra's boss, based on how deferential she was acting around her. The other was a young blonde lady wearing armor. She looked sorta familiar for some reason.

"This is the patient I spoke about," Myra guided them towards me, and then took a short step back.

"Hello there. My name is Eleanor. I'm the High Bishop of this temple. Could you please tell me your name?" She spoke with a peaceful voice while sporting a gentle smile. Just hearing her voice made me feel calmer. I instinctively felt like I could trust her, maybe due to her grandmotherly sort of aura?

⊹─────────────────⊹
> Name: Eleanor LV: 62 
> Class: Arch Priest 
> Title: High Bishop of Tutoria Temple
> Association: Tutoria Temple
> HP: X/X MP: X/X
⊹─────────────────⊹

Once again this damn blue screen shows up. I give it a cursory glance and then focus back on Eleanor.

"I'm Jay."

"Hello Jay. Myra has briefed me on a few things about your current situation. It seems she has been unable to cast healing magic upon you. Is that correct?"

"I can't say. I wasn't awake when she did that."

"Understood. Would you permit me to cast some healing magic upon you? I know several more healing spells that could prove to be useful."

"Sure. Have a go at it."

It seems like Eleanor, after listening to Myra's account of what happened, came to the conclusion that Myra's attempt at healing was the issue. She probably wants to test it out herself to confirm the situation.

Hey, good for me. If she succeeds, then I'll be healed. Otherwise, it's the same. As a bonus, I'll actually be able to see different kinds of so called healing magic!

She walked over towards me, and with a short "Excuse me", she carefully unwrapped the bandages around my leg.

Oooffh, my leg looks raw. It's heavily bruised and swollen, with cuts littered all across. To imagine that this is considered a "lucky escape" shows how dire my stumbling into the goblin camp was.

Eleanor placed her hands over my injured leg and chanted a short word:

"Heal."

A light green glow blossomed within her hands and washed over to envelop my right leg. It shone brightly once it completely covered the injured area and slowly faded away.

That looked cool as heck! Alas, nothing changed. My leg looks exactly as raw as before.

"It looks like it did not have the desired effect. Do you feel like your pain has subsided by any amount?"

"No. My leg hurts just as bad."

"It was the same when I cast Heal as well. It did not show any effect." Myra added from behind.

"I see. It could be that the spell was not powerful enough, or that your injury does not respond to the Heal spell."

It looked like Eleanor was trying to make an argument as to why the spell did not work, but she hardly looks convinced herself.

"Alright, I shall cast a more potent spell this time."

She once again placed her hands over my right leg and chanted,

"Greater Heal."

A similar glow enveloped my leg, but it shone even more brightly this time. The end result was the same unfortunately. Yo, this is concerning. Are these people not capable of fixing me?

Eleanor placed a hand on her face and sighed. "It looks like the spell is going through just fine, but it does not look like it is having any effect. How troubling."

"High Bishop, could you try a different kind of spell, other than healing?" Myra piped up. "As I am still an apprentice, Heal is the only spell available to me."

"A valid suggestion." Eleanor nodded. She closed her eyes and looked like she was ruminating on a few thoughts.

"I shall cast Blessing of Titan," she announced once her mind was set.

"Please wait a moment," the armored lady spoke out at once. "Isn't a 4th grade spell an overkill for this situation?"

"I have deemed that any spell lower than this won't have an effect, thus a waste of my mana."

"But it might prove to be dangerous!"

"Your concern is unwarranted. Or are you implying that you can't handle a commoner who was beat up by some goblins?"

"No, ma'am."

"Then please stay silent, and refrain from further objections." Eleanor glared at the lady, which shut her up, and approached me with a smile that did not look gentle at all. Ooh, what kind of magic is this crazy lady going to cast on me next?

"Shall we proceed, Jay?"

"Yes, ma'am," I squeaked, looking away from her.

Eleanor brought her hands together in a praying pose. Light beads started forming and gathering around her. More and more light beads glittered around her as she continued praying.

She opened her eyes as she finished praying, and pointed her hands upwards and chanted:

"Blessings of Titan."

The light around pooled up and gathered into a ball above me. Then it broke apart and the light gently sprinkled all over me. It felt very calming.

"How do you feel?" Eleanor asked once the light completely disappeared.

I closed my eyes, trying to recall the beautiful and mystical lights. "I feel like I was healed spiritually."

"So, does that mean you do not feel any different?"

"Unfortunately, yes. I don't feel different physically. Seems like the magic spell failed? Thanks for trying though."

"No — the fault lies elsewhere. Your body simply does not respond. In all my years of practice, I have never encountered anything like it. I'm afraid the answers to your condition lie well beyond my understanding." she sighed.

There was a look of admiration on Myra's face, while the armored lady looked a little terrified.

"That concludes my examination. This young lady here is Captain Iris, and she has a few questions for you," Eleanor said as she stepped aside.

Captain Iris...

Wait, isn't that the name of the knight who saved me from the monsters?
