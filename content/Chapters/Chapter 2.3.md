"Hello, I'm Iris. Current Captain of the Force under Lord Iguma. I would like to speak with you for a moment if you are feeling good enough."

"Hello Captain Iris, I'm Jay. I've heard from Myra that you were the one who rescued me a few nights ago. Thank you very much. I almost saw death itself waving at me from the other side of the River Styx."

"River Styx, I've never heard of such a river. Where is it located?"

Ah, communication barrier with other-worlders. I need to be more mindful with my references.

"It's not a real river, it's just a figure of speech. Don't mind it."

"Hmm, a rather curious figure of speech. I've never heard anyone use it before."

"hahaha."

I scratched my head with my finger as I looked away awkwardly. Seems like I raised a flag of clear suspicion with her. Her eyes are scrutinizing me even more keenly now. Dammit, stupid me. This is not the kind of red flag I should be raising. Must be all greens only. Green flags are the way of being a gentleman.

"Those were some scary monsters that day. You must be really strong for fighting them off." I tried to distract her.

No kidding, from my last awake memory of that night all I remember is someone flashing a blade and cleanly decapitating the monster in front of me in one clean swing. If that was her, ooffh, she's a baddie for sure.

"Goblins? Scary? Anyone can take them down with ease. They are just some small fry."

........

You can't see it, but this is my stunned face. Those hideous looking monsters are... small fry? What kind of devilish world have I ended up in?

"Ah, there were quite a few of them gathered at that time. Goblins are notorious for ganging up on helpless prey. I can see how that would be bothersome." she nodded to herself.

I just nodded along. What else could I say?

"Closer to the gates, you usually only find solitary ones. One has to venture much further deep into the forest to encounter the camps. Citizens are warned to not enter such depths of the forest." she elaborated.

Her eyes sharpened as if she suddenly noticed something strange. Uhh, what's happening?

"Unless one clears out the mobs that they face along the route, you cannot reach the area we found you at. You were there all alone. Naturally, the question follows - How did you reach the Deep Woods on your own?" she asked.

"I don't know, I was there suddenly when I opened my eyes."

"You found yourself in the Deep Woods accidentally? One way to reach the Deep Woods is to hire an escort. However, I've not heard of any escort missions to the Deep Woods of late, neither have I heard of any missing clients from the current escort missions," she mumbled to herself.

Oh no, this is starting to sound a bit ominous now.

"Could you have been kidnapped by bandits? There isn't much bandit activity in these parts, and surely they have no reason to carry an unconscious person into the Deep Woods. Hmm..."

She squinted at me, with clear suspicion in her eyes.

"Who are you? Where are you from?"

......

Okay! Ladies and Gentlemen, this is the biggest trap one faces at the start of their isekai journey. How should you answer the question: Who are you?

If you lie and they catch it, you will be placed under massive suspicion. Whereas if you tell the truth, they will cut you up into tiny pieces and examine your body for research. So what is the best response in this situation?

"I'll be honest, I haven't been entirely truthful with you folks."

That's right, honesty is the best policy. I'm in an unknown world, and I will need a lot of help to adjust. It is in my best interests to be open. Maybe they know a way of helping me get back to Earth?

Look at that. All of them are looking at me with such pure gazes. Gazes of pure suspicion.

"What are you hiding from us?" Iris questioned in a far deeper tone, brows furrowed.

"I ..... I am not....."

I lowered my head, unable to complete. Come on, you can do it. Clenching my fists, I looked back into their eyes with renewed courage.

"I do not remember who I am, or who I was. I have lost my memory."

!!!!

"I don't remember anything before the incident with those monsters — they are called goblins, right? Those things attacking me is the last thing I remember."
.......

"I don't recall anything except my name. I don't recall where I'm from, or why I'm here. I don't even recall my parents' names or faces, if I'm being honest."

I looked down again, unable to continue any further. A somber mood fell over the room.

"That's... so sad," Myra spoke with tears in her eyes.

Gotcha! Ofcourse I ain't so naive to trust them directly. Better to throw them into confusion and gain some time to gather some info about this place.

"What about those places you mentioned before? You said something about a river named Styx. Where did you hear about that river?" Iris questioned. Seems like someone is not convinced yet.

"I don't recall where I've heard about it. I don't even know if it is a real river or something I just made up on the spot. I just spoke what came to my mind."

"Why were you not upfront about your condition?" she persevered.

"I mean, my mind is all hazy and I feel like a mess. I still have PTSD from those monsters attacking me, you know?"

"PTSD? What is that?"

"I meant to say that I am still traumatized from that incident and haven't had time to collect my thoughts... I was hoping to make sense of the situation myself before you started questioning me. I do not have answers to what I don't know. Sorry, I can't be of more help here."

......

"Could you please let me rest for a while? My body is still very sore, and my head is in a fog. I will let you know if I recall anything related to the incident or what occurred prior to it." saying so, I dug under my blanket and closed my eyes. I was making it clear that I was not willing to have further conversation.

Iris stewed with her thoughts before turning to the other ladies in the room. At least she is civil enough to not rip the blanket off a patient to satisfy her curiosity.

"High Bishop, what is your assessment of his condition?" Iris asked.

"It is difficult to ascertain. Memory loss is somewhat of a rare condition, so there isn't much known about it." Eleanor replied, as she massaged her temples with her fingers.

Even back on Earth, nobody really understood how the brain works. That after all the scientific breakthroughs we had. No way these country looking plebs would know anything solid about it.

"Do we at least know what could cause such a condition?" Iris further probed.

"Temporary memory loss has been rumoured to occur when one is struck on their head with blunt force. It does fit his condition if we take a look at his injuries. However, since any form of healing has not been effective on him, I cannot say for sure. We do not know if the ineffectiveness of healing has resulted in this condition, or if they are symptoms of a larger curse."

"A curse that conveniently erases his memory of how he reached the Deep woods?" Iris quiped.

"I would suggest we keep him here under further observation. Hopefully his condition gets better over time. I shall let you know of any changes. So shall we let him rest for now?" Eleanor tried to smooth over. 

"If you say so, ma'am," Iris finally relented. Much thanks for the backup, High Bishop!

All of them soon moved to leave the room, though I caught Iris giving me one last scrutinizing look as she left.
...........

Finally that damn interrogation is over with. What's with that woman? Why was she still giving me that scary look as she left?

Great, just what I was lacking in my grand adventure so far - an uptight knight digging into my business.
