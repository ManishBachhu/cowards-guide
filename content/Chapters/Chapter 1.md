I open my eyes.

I'm sprawled out on my back on bare earth, in the middle of a forest, staring up at the night sky. Dark. Scary. Creepy.

_Why am I here?_

Just moments ago I was cozy in my room, having just booted up a new game I wanted to try. The screen flashed so brightly I had to shut my eyes. My first instinct was to fire off an angry email to whatever ridiculous engineer designed that loading screen.

Yet when I open my eyes, I'm in the middle of nowhere. This makes no sense.

I get up, brush myself off, and feel my pockets. Nothing. Zip. Nada.

Makes sense, I suppose. I was just chilling in my room with my phone on charge and everything else within reach. Why would I have anything in my pockets? Luckily, I'm dressed modestly — I share my dorm room. I cringed imagining my fate had I been whisked away at night, in my sleep. Summer nights don't take kindly to layers.

I trudge around in the dark, trying to make sense of where I am, taking careful steps on bare feet to avoid the rocks scattered around. Not having my phone sucks. I could have called a helpline, checked my GPS, or at the very least used the flashlight.

Then I spot something — a dim light flickering in the distance.

As I move toward it, it grows brighter, warm and orange. A campfire, maybe? That would be lucky. Someone who could give me a ride back to the city.

With such positive thoughts, I push through the foliage and call out.

"Hey! Could you help me out? I'm lost and need a ride to—"

I break into the campsite and stop dead.

Sitting around the fire aren't friendly hikers. They're creatures — short, with arms and legs, but that's where any resemblance to human ends. Large, pointy ears sprout from the tops of their heads. Huge, red, beady eyes that somehow seem to glow. Sharp fangs protrude from their mouths.

Truly grotesque.

They'd been munching on meat and cackling when I burst in. Now, all of them fall silent, every pair of eerie eyes locking onto me.

Bloodlust. I can feel it radiating off all of them. Unless I get out of here, I'm going to be their next snack.

I inch backwards, trying to make myself smaller, quieter, less visible. They seem merely curious about whatever had stumbled into their dinner — hence the silence. Then one of them rises from its rock, wrapping a thick, gnarled fist around a club.

Intentions clear. I run.

All hell breaks loose. They fall over each other scrambling after me, and their clumsiness buys me a brief head start. It doesn't last. Barefoot in the dark, I'm stumbling and weaving while they move light and agile, already closing the gap.

They're coming from all directions.

This is their hunting ground. I am their prey.

I run as hard as I can, already out of breath.

It takes a single rock.

I go down hard. One of them is on me instantly, club swinging down — I throw my left arm up to block it and take the blow flush on the elbow. The arm goes useless almost immediately.

I cry out, but the impact throws it off balance just enough. I scramble back up and run.

The others have caught up. One swings from the right — I block it with my good arm. But I have nothing for the one coming from the other side. It launches itself at me like a feral animal and sinks its teeth into my leg.

I yelp, kick it off, and have nothing left.

I lose my balance and go down again.

Half a dozen of them pile on. Clubs come down on my back, my arms. One lands directly across my face.

I'm screaming. Bleeding from everywhere. Running out of fight.

At this rate I'm not just going to die here — I'm going to be eaten alive, picked clean before anyone even knows I'm gone.

_What had I done to deserve this?_ One moment I was peacefully gaming in my room. The next I'm here, in this ridiculous place, being beaten to death by creatures that shouldn't exist. The thoughts feel distant, like they belong to someone else — swallowed up by the pain and the dark closing in around me.

Then — a flash. Bright and sudden, cutting through the chaos like a blade.

One of the monsters drops. Its head is gone before I even register the sound. The others scatter, shrieking in a pitch that splits my ears.

I catch a glimpse — blonde hair, armor, a weapon still singing from the swing.

Then the darkness wins, and pulls me under.
