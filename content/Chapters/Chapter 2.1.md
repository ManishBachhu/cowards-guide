I woke up to an unfamiliar ceiling. _I'm still alive ?!_

Was that a bizarre nightmare? Ooofff, the aches all over my body immediately signal a 'NO'. 

I crane my head around to get a better look. I'm all wrapped up in bandages — a bit excessively, in fact. Not complaining, though. I'm somehow still alive even after what happened last night. Is it  morning already? What time, or better yet, what day is it? In fact, before all of that — where in the world am I? For that matter, what world am I in?

I look around and spot a person arranging some things on the table across the room. She seems to have sensed that I'm awake, and approaches me.

"Please don't move around yet, I'm not done wrapping the bandages yet." Even more bandages?

"Urgh. I don't feel good at all. Every inch of my body is screaming in pain."

"That is understandable after what you have been through. I've heard that a gang of goblins attacked you two days ago. There are severe bruises all over your body." She started working on some bandages around my arm, but paused suddenly. She was staring at my arm with a defeated look. 

"I tried applying some healing magic to relieve some of your pain, but ..... it seems I was inadequate. My healing showed no effect at all. I even double checked the prayer I was using. I did what I could and wrapped the injured areas with some bandages, but they seem to have hardly healed at all." she sighed and started wrapping again.

Based on her mannerisms — the way she is checking my forehead for temperature and inspecting the state of the bandages — she seems to be a nurse. But hang on, there are some deeply concerning things about what she just said!

"Hold on! Stop! Stop!" I frantically stop her.

"Hmm? Are the bandages too tight?"

She seems to have misunderstood my pain as physical pain in response to what she was checking on my bandage, but this is an emotional distress in regards to her words.

"No forget that! What do you mean healing magic?! Actually, first of all, where am I? What is this place?!"

"You're in Tutoria's temple."

"Temple? This is not a hospital? And where the hell is Tutoria at? Is this England or France or something?"

"England? France? I've never heard of those places before."

"Have you heard of Earth at least?"

"No, I haven't," she replied with a truly puzzled face.

I feel like I'm drowning. I had a gut feeling that this place might not be Earth, after facing those monsters. However, to actually hear the confirmation is devastating.

People in fictional novels may be happy that they have been teleported to a new mystical world, but I assure you there is nothing funny — this is not a joyful occasion. It is terrifying.

"Are you all right?" she asked me, looking at my crestfallen state.

"Not one bit. I have no clue how I ended up here!"

"At the temple? You were brought in by Captain Iris and her patrol squad. They were passing through the area when they came across you and the goblin herd. Fortunate timing, truly — another few minutes and I suspect the outcome would have been very different." She pauses, smoothing out one of the bandages. "They carried you here themselves."

"I didn't mean how I was brought to the temple. I'm asking how I ended up in that forest in the first place?!"

"Do you not remember what happened to you before the goblins attacked you?"

"No, I do. I started playing this new game while in my dorm room, and the screen went all white. Then suddenly, I woke up in the middle of the forest!"

"A game? What kind of game were you playing?"

"A newly released video game, Path of Pain."

"A video game? What does this 'video game' mean?"

My heart sank further. They do not have video games here? How am I going to live from now on?

"You have never heard of video games? What about a TV? Cellphone? Microwave? Refrigerator?"

"Umm, I have never heard of any of them."

"Oh my god! Are there no electronics at all here?" I held my head, unable to handle the severity of this new reality. Ouch. Just raising my hands sent a wave of pain through my body. Somehow, that was the least depressing part of the current situation.

"Please take some rest and wait here. I will fetch someone who could maybe help you better," she said as she started moving towards the door. What caught my eye, however, was that a blue screen popped up in front of her as she started walking away.

"Wait, hold on! What is that screen? Where did it come from?!"

"This? It's a normal status screen," she said with a puzzled expression, as if I was asking the most obvious question in the world.

"A status screen? How is that possible? Show me."

She seemed a bit troubled with my request but obliged nonetheless. With a wave of her hand, the floating blue screen moved towards me.

"Wow, this sure is something else."

⊹─────────────────⊹ 
Name: Myra 
LV: 8
Class: Apprentice Priest 
Association: Temple of Tutoria
HP: X/X　　MP: X/X 
⊹─────────────────⊹

I am staring at a legitimate status screen. A screen that should never exist outside a video game, but fantastically, is right in front of me now. It not only shows her name, but it also has level, class, and other information. 

"Wow, how did it appear? Where did it come from?"

"Where? What do you mean? Doesn't everyone have one?"

"For real? Do I have one as well?"

"Yes? Could you not just summon it?"

She mentioned it as if she were explaining it to a child.

"How?! How can I summon it?" 'SUMMON STATUS SCREEN!' I yelled excitedly.

"Please don't raise your voice in that manner here. You don't need to say it out loud. You just need to think of it and it will appear. Though, hand gestures do help sometimes."

"Status screen, I summon you!" I yelled anyway.

........

Nothing happened.

"Open status screen!" I said while waving my hand. Still nothing.

.........

"Khul ja sim sim. Khul ja status screen!" I waved even more frantically than usual.

................

No dice.

No matter what I did, what I said, or however hard I yelled, nothing showed up.

"Why?! What am I doing wrong?" I'm on the verge of tears now.

"I'm not sure myself." Myra looked extremely perplexed. "I have never seen anyone try this hard and not be successful in summoning a status screen before, not even young children. It always appears unless one willingly does not want to display it to the other person." 

"Does it look like I'm trying to hide it?"

"No no, I did not mean it that way."

I stop scowling at her, it's not her fault her anyways. But God, I am so despondent. I am physically, emotionally, and now even mentally broken down.

"Whatever, I'm going back to sleep." I turn away and bury my head in my hands.

"Yes, I would advise so too. Please take some rest. I shall consult with the high bishop meanwhile. She is knowledgeable on lots of illnesses, so perhaps she would have a better idea about your condition. This means I have to report that my healing magic was a complete failure earlier ...., but getting you the right treatment is the higher priority" She sighed, while nodding to herself. After placing a blanket over me, she walks out, leaving me alone with my thoughts.

Why can't I open the damn status screen? Based on Myra's words, I seem to be the only one who can't. She seems to have no idea why, but could it be .... because I'm originally from Earth and not born in this world, I can't use magic of this world? No, no no. It's too early to conclude.

Then there's that healing magic she kept talking about, and those goblins in that forest.

This seems to be a fantasy world from the looks of it. And I appear to be the only person in it who got absolutely none of the perks ??!

Have I been totally screwed over?
