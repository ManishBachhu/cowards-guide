For the next few days, I did not have any more visitors. Myra was the only one tasked with treating me and tending to my wounds. I asked her if it was too much work handling my bandages on her own; she replied it wasn't that much extra work. Apparently, she is the lowest ranked priest in the temple, and an apprentice at that, so all of her seniors tended to dump all the menial chores on her. Temple hierarchy is as brutal as a corporate ladder, it seems.

One day she brought me a small glass vial with some kind of medicine in it. It was pale red and had a slight glow to it. Myra said it was medicine that could help with my condition. I gratefully accepted it and drank the contents of the vial. The next day I was feeling much better physically and the pain subsided a lot.

When I thanked Myra for bringing me the medicine and mentioned that it was effective, she said it was fine — she already understood that the potion did not work, so I didn't need to try to make her feel better. Umm, okay?

Whether it was the medicine or it was my body finally making some progress on healing itself, I finally started feeling much better. My appetite increased and it did not hurt anymore while moving around. I was even able to walk around and fetch water myself now. It only seems like a matter of time until I'm back to decently fit again.

As I was basking in the delightful evening sunlight by the window, Myra timidly approached me. It seemed she had something to say to me.

"Hey Myra, thanks again for all the help. I feel much better than when I came in!"

"Yes, it would seem that you will be all good to leave in a couple of days."

That sounds like good news, right? Why is her face still looking concerned?

"Would you mind if I asked you a personal question?"

"Not that I remember much, but shoot it anyways."

"Do you have any money on your person or with anyone close to you to pay for your treatment here?"

Payment? Money? What?

"I need to pay for the treatment?"

"Yes. It is a custom to donate to the temple for all the treatments and blessings one receives."

Aw crap. I did not expect reality to spring such a trap on me. I was so caught up with those monsters and the lady knight's suspicions that the topic of money completely slipped from my mind. Even the modern societies back on Earth rarely offer free healthcare. It was dumb of me to expect communism from this world.

I was cradling my head and couldn't even respond to Myra when she asked, "Will you be alright?" This world probably won't accept the paper currency from Earth. In fact I probably don't even have that, cause I don't have my wallet itself. The situation is despair-inducing - forget the cost of the treatment, how am I gonna pay for my necessities from now on?

All of my thoughts went to the darkest of conclusions - getting kidnapped and being sold off as a slave to settle my debts, getting worked to the bone, or getting my organs harvested. I literally have no security in this new world.

I looked up pitifully at Myra and croaked, "Please help me find a job" with tears in my eyes.

She sighed and said that she predicted that this would be the case when I said I had lost my memories. She didn't bring it up before because she didn't want to disturb my mental state during the treatment.

Myra said she'd speak with Eleanor to check if there were any jobs I could do at the temple. Outsiders are usually not considered for work at the temple, but maybe they could at least find something temporary. That naturally led to the question of what my skills were, what kind of jobs I could handle.

"Do you remember what skills you had? This would have been easy to figure out if your status screen opened, but we have had no luck with that so far."

It was a good question to ponder - what could I realistically do well in this world? I was basically a business student back on Earth. I dreamt of starting a small company after college. That dream is probably dead in the water, and right now I have bigger problems. All I knew was some math and a few basic survival skills for living on my own. Would these be sufficient here?

"Umm, I can do math well. I could probably do some cleaning or laundry? Oh! I can handle myself in the kitchen decently well as I cooked for myself, I think."

"So you have "Cook", "Clean" and "Calculate" skills? Around here, they might prove to be helpful. Incidentally, do you recall what level these skills are at? "

"Skill? Level? What are you speaking about?"

With a flick of her finger, Myra brought up her status screen and pointed at the "Skills" section.

⊹─────────────────⊹ 
     SKILLS 
- Prayer Lv. 4
- Healing Lv. 1 
- Patient-care Lv. 3
- Cleaning Lv. 2
⊹─────────────────⊹ 

"I'm still an apprentice, so I don't have many useful or high-level skills yet," she said, looking a bit embarrassed.

I didn't particularly care about the levels themselves, but the fact that even basic tasks like praying and cleaning were listed as skills was alarming.

"Is it absolutely necessary to possess a skill to be qualified for a job?"

"Yes, it is essential to obtain all the skills necessary for the role before you can start working. In the worst case, one can join as an apprentice to learn these skills. As you can see, I'm working as an apprentice priest to learn more healing skills."

I am a normal human though? I don't have these so-called "skills." Will I actually be able to land a job and do any work in this world?
